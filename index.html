02 — How I think about craft

A call is fine until one of three numbers isn't.

Voice quality is not a feeling. It is packet loss, jitter and round-trip time, measured on the handset while the call is still happening. These are the thresholds I wrote into the Maqsam softphone's quality reporting — cross one and the call gets flagged, with the cause attached.

Per-call sampling · reported with the call record Warn zone shaded
Packet loss0.0 %
warn above 3.0 %scale 5 %
Jitter0 ms
warn above 30 msscale 50 ms
Round trip0 ms
warn above 400 msscale 600 ms

Sampled in Dart from getStats on the Janus path, with an E-model MOS estimate, and read from Twilio's native MOS on the other — then normalised into one report. The same change touched Dart, Java, Swift and Ruby.

05 — The route here

Five years, in the order they happened.

2021

Learned Flutter, then shipped it

Picked up Dart on my own and took a freelance project all the way to production with it. First time the thing I had been reading about had a real user on the other end, and the only person to ask was me.

2022

One codebase, three platforms

Built an e-learning app with progress tracking and achievements, hardened against screen capture because the content was the product, then carried it to desktop from the same Flutter source. It was later licensed on to other institutions.

2023

Realtime, both sides of it

Rider and driver apps for a ride-hailing platform: live location tracking, WebSocket chat, Maps services, and work that continues while the app is closed. Where I learned that a flaky connection is the normal case, not the edge case.

2024

Led a build end to end

Ran a team delivering a medical insurance app for an enterprise client abroad, from first scoping through release. Learned that the plan and the people are the same job, not two.

2025

Into telephony

Joined Maqsam and took over its Flutter softphone. Rebuilt eight CRM and helpdesk integrations in the customer portal as well, which pulled me properly into Rails and Vue.

2026

Both halves at once

Shipped call-quality reporting spanning Dart, Java, Swift and Ruby — then built a full multi-vendor marketplace solo, API through storefront, to prove the backend half was not borrowed.
